# Settings wallpaper assets

Selected by the user: Golden Gate Light and Golden Gate Dark.

Source archive: https://512pixels.net/projects/default-mac-wallpapers-in-5k/

Original downloads:
- Light: https://media.512pixels.net/downloads/macos-wallpapers-6k/27-Golden-Gate.png
- Dark: https://media.512pixels.net/downloads/macos-wallpapers-6k/27-Golden-Gate-Dark.png

Bundled copies are resized to a maximum dimension of 1920 and encoded as JPEG
at quality 88. Appearance follows the existing application day/night mode.
The source archive is a download source; it does not establish redistribution rights.

SettingsBackdrop caches a small blurred copy per appearance. Cards draw its
corresponding screen region plus a translucent material tint. Pre-draw position
tracking keeps the wallpaper aligned while the contents scroll. Text and controls
are not blurred. No CarPlay frame capture or changes to lyrics are involved.
